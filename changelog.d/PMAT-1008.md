**Changelog entries and roadmap rows are per-PR fragment files, assembled at release (PMAT-1008, #1007).**
A PR adds `changelog.d/<id>.md` and `docs/roadmaps/fragments/<id>.yaml` instead of editing `CHANGELOG.md` and `roadmap.yaml`, so concurrent PRs stop conflicting on the same lines. `make assemble-fragments` folds them in at the release cut and refuses duplicate ids; `make check-fragments` runs the check.
