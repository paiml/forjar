# Quorum evidence — PMAT-210 — the claims put to the lanes

1. Deferring an S1 to a named release is the right disposition when the release carrying it is already tagged, published and pinned.
2. The disposition is recorded where a reader of the release's own triage will find it, and the pre-existing claim about the cut is not falsified by the addition.
3. The narrowing in the receipt — not rendering, not the hash of a literal content block, not local write-then-read — is supported by measurement rather than by an absence of failure.
4. The branch touches only what a `kind:triage` branch may touch.
5. `pmat work validate` passes and every id named resolves in the roadmap.

Claims 1, 2 and 4 were confirmed. Claim 3 was refuted on the evidence as first written and re-established only after the control probe. Claim 5 is mechanical and was re-run by the orchestrator, not accepted from a lane.
