# PMAT-540 — the claims put to the round

Gate A gains one arm: a merged PR filed under a ticket its own merge commit
contradicts is named and red, from `TRAILER_FLOOR` onward.

1. The arm is sound: no PR shape reproduces PR #532's misattribution and still
   passes, `git merge-base --is-ancestor` is used with the right argument order,
   and every failure path is UNMEASURED rather than a silent skip.
2. The floor is where it must be: at #532's own merge commit and no later, and a
   floor this repository does not carry exempts nothing.
3. The trailer is read by the LINE because git's own parser cannot see it on the
   commit the rule exists for.
4. Each of the seven cases dies when the arm is removed, or is killed by its own
   targeted mutation.
5. The fixture reproduces the shape that makes git's parser blind.
6. The receipt, the log, CLAUDE.md and the skill describe what the script does,
   and their numbers are what the tools printed.

Each lane had a different brief — the arm and the floor; the cases and the
fixture; the documents and the numbers — and all three ended with the standing
instruction: *quote any sentence a reader could check and find false, say what
is actually true, and cite where you measured it.*
